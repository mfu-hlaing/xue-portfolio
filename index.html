About

I grew up in Mogok, Myanmar, and started learning Chinese young enough that HSK Level 3 came at nine years old — 297 out of 300.

Since June 2020 I have taught Mandarin to younger students at ESC Chinese Language Training Center, the school where I had been a student since childhood — first in the classroom, then online.

Today I am studying for a Bachelor of Economics at Mae Fah Luang University in Chiang Rai, taught entirely in English under the philosophy “Economics for Future Society” — a 123-credit degree weighted toward mathematics, statistics and econometrics. Most recently I interned with Anjia Property in Pattaya, presenting pool villas, branded residences and beachfront condos on camera.

I am looking for graduate work where the economics and the languages pull in the same direction: market and trade research, investment analysis, or property consulting across the Thailand–Myanmar–China corridor.
