About

I am an economics undergraduate at Mae Fah Luang University, working across the Thailand–Myanmar–China corridor in four languages — Burmese, English, Thai and Mandarin.

Since June 2020 I have taught Mandarin at ESC Chinese Language Training Center, in the classroom and online, preparing students for HSK proficiency exams. Five years of client-facing teaching in a second language is where my communication skills come from.

My degree is taught entirely in English under the philosophy “Economics for Future Society” — a 123-credit programme weighted toward mathematics, statistics and econometrics, alongside applied data analysis. Most recently I interned with Anjia Property in Pattaya, presenting pool villas, branded residences and beachfront condos on camera to Chinese-, Thai-, Burmese- and English-speaking buyers.

I am looking for full-time roles in market and trade research, investment analysis, or property consulting — work where economic method and multilingual client communication pull in the same direction. I am available to start now.
